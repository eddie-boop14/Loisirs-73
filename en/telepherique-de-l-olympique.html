What is Olympique Cable Car

The Olympique cable car is the Val-d'Isère lift that takes mountain bikes. It is a 3S bicable cable car per its technical record — not a gondola, despite common usage.

It runs on two pricing regimes: free for pedestrians per Val d'Isère, charged for mountain bikes at €23 a day and €84 a week. The advertised price is therefore not the price of the ride: it is the price of the bike.

It runs from 09:45 to 16:45 without a break — worth noting in a département where several lifts stop for an hour at midday.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the Olympique a gondola or a cable car?

Per its technical record it is a Doppelmayr 3S bicable cable car. The word "gondola" circulates but does not match the technical designation.

Can you take a mountain bike up?

Yes, it is the only one of the two lifts documented here that accepts them: the Solaise gondola takes walkers and paragliders only.

How much does the ride cost?

Nothing for a pedestrian, per Val d'Isère. The bike pass is €23 a day and €84 a week: those figures apply to bikes only.

What are the hours?

09:45 to 16:45, with no midday break — the sources state this explicitly.

How high does it go?

No source consulted publishes it, even though the lift is presented as access to the bike park and the Pisaillas glacier.
