What is Glaciers Gondola

The Glaciers gondola extends the Roche de Mio gondola and reaches, per La Plagne's official page, "over 3,000 metres in less than 15 minutes". One secondary source claims 3,080 m, another describes a link between 2,700 and 3,000 m: the operator itself declines to give a precise figure.

Two practical points. It is closed on Saturdays, like the Roche de Mio that feeds it — checking only one of the two is not enough. And it is pedestrians only: no skiers, no mountain bikes.

It replaced the Bellecôte gondola, and the Bellecôte glacier is no longer operated: any page describing summer skiing there is out of date.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When does the Glaciers gondola run?

From 4 July to 29 August 2026, 09:35 to 16:40, on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday. Closed Saturdays, and subject to favourable weather.

How high does it go?

The official page says "over 3,000 metres" without a precise figure. One secondary source claims 3,080 m, another describes a link stopping at 3,000 m. Nothing is arbitrated.

Can you ride up directly from the resort?

No. The ride and its duration are counted from Roche de Mio: you must first take the Roche de Mio gondola from Plagne Bellecôte.

Can you take a mountain bike up?

No, it is reserved for pedestrians. No skiers, no mountain bikes.

Can you ski on the glacier in summer?

No, the Bellecôte glacier is no longer operated. The Glaciers gondola replaced the Bellecôte gondola; pages describing summer skiing there are out of date.

What does it cost?

Access: Paid.
