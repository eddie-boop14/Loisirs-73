# Loisirs 73 — heroes still needed (13)

**17 of 30 sourced.** This is the remaining list only — the full record, including the 17 already wired and their licences, is in `PHOTO-SHOPPING-LIST.md` and `photo-credits.json`.

## What has worked so far

- **Florian Pépellin** has supplied 7 of the 17. He photographs Savoie's lift infrastructure systematically — [his uploads](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:ListFiles/Florian_P%C3%A9pellin) are worth browsing directly for the lifts below.
- **Category pages beat title search.** `Category:Vanoise Express` and `Category:Télécabine d'Orelle-Caron` both produced hits that a plain search missed. Category links are in the table.
- **Check the date on anything mechanical.** One file was refused this round for pre-dating the machine it appeared to show.

## The 13

### `col-des-saisies`

**Col des Saisies** · Hauteluce (73620)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Col+des+Saisies+Hauteluce&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Col_des_Saisies)

Hauteluce. Inhabited and open all year, so summer or winter both work.

### `col-du-chaussy`

**Col du Chaussy** · Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne (73300)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Col+du+Chaussy+Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Col_du_Chaussy)

The famous object is the **Lacets de Montvernier** below the col — the stacked hairpins. Either works; a lacets shot is the stronger image and is what the page leads on.

### `col-du-mollard`

**Col du Mollard** · Albiez-Montrond (73300)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Col+du+Mollard+Albiez-Montrond&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Col_du_Mollard)

1 638 m, Albiez-Montrond, known for its 45 hairpins. A lake sits beside the col.

### `cormet-d-areches`

**Cormet d'Arêches** · Beaufort (73270)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Cormet+d%27Ar%C3%AAches+Beaufort&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Cormet_d%27Ar%C3%AAches)

2 107 m, Beaufortain. **Not** the Cormet de Roselend, 139 m lower and a different road. The last 3.2 km are an unsurfaced track, so shots often show a rough road — that is correct here.

### `cormet-de-roselend`

**Cormet de Roselend** · Beaufort (73270)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Cormet+de+Roselend+Beaufort&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Cormet_de_Roselend)

The pass itself at 1 968 m. Beware the **Lac de Roselend** below it — beautiful, frequently photographed, and a different place. The col is bare: a car park and a view, no buildings.

### `signal-de-bisanne`

**Signal de Bisanne** · Hauteluce (73620)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=Signal+de+Bisanne+Hauteluce&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Signal_de_Bisanne)

The summit at 1 930 m above Les Saisies — **not** the resort at 1 723 m, which is where the 2016 Tour stage was judged. Paragliding launch site, so aerial shots exist.

### `telecabine-de-champagny`

**Télécabine de Champagny** · Champagny-en-Vanoise (73350)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=T%C3%A9l%C3%A9cabine+de+Champagny+Champagny-en-Vanoise&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:T%C3%A9l%C3%A9cabine_de_Champagny)

Champagny-en-Vanoise, a TCD8/10. Gateway to the Vanoise.

### `telecabine-de-la-roche-de-mio`

**Télécabine de la Roche de Mio** · La Plagne Tarentaise (73210)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=T%C3%A9l%C3%A9cabine+de+la+Roche+de+Mio+La+Plagne+Tarentaise&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:T%C3%A9l%C3%A9cabine_de_la_Roche_de_Mio)

⛔ **Must be dated after December 2025.** The machine was replaced that month; an April 2025 photo was already refused because it shows the removed Poma TCD6 (6 cabins) rather than the current 10-seat gondola. Check the EXIF date, not just the caption.

### `telecabine-des-glaciers`

**Télécabine des Glaciers** · La Plagne Tarentaise (73210)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=T%C3%A9l%C3%A9cabine+des+Glaciers+La+Plagne+Tarentaise&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:T%C3%A9l%C3%A9cabine_des_Glaciers)

### `telepherique-de-l-olympique`

**Téléphérique de l'Olympique** · Val-d'Isère (73150)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=T%C3%A9l%C3%A9ph%C3%A9rique+de+l%27Olympique+Val-d%27Is%C3%A8re&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:T%C3%A9l%C3%A9ph%C3%A9rique_de_l%27Olympique)

**Téléphérique de la Cime Caron** · Les Belleville (73440)

[Media search](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=T%C3%A9l%C3%A9ph%C3%A9rique+de+la+Cime+Caron+Les+Belleville&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image) · [Category (try)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:T%C3%A9l%C3%A9ph%C3%A9rique_de_la_Cime_Caron)

## Reminders

- Licence must be **CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or public domain**, and the author must be recordable.
- **Landscape**, ideally ≥ 1600 px wide.
- **The machine, not the view from it.** A lift fiche wants the cabins or a station. A col fiche wants the col, not the valley below. Two of the 17 already wired miss this and are flagged for replacement.
