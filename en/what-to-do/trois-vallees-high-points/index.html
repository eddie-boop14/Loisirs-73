Les Trois Vallées up high: the reachable high points

Saulire, Mont Vallon, Pointe de la Masse, Cime Caron: the Trois Vallées summits you can reach without walking, and the days the lifts do not run.

How this selection is made — Compiled from our verified fact sheets: this page's contents are exactly the output of the stated selector, never a hand-written list. It updates whenever a sheet changes.

The selection · 6

Cime Caron Cable Car

Les Belleville
Wheelchair accessWinter ✓

From 2,320 m to about 3,190 m above Val Thorens. New cabins since 22 November 2025. Summer 2026: 4 July – 28 August, 9:45am–4:15pm, last ascent 3:45pm.

See details →

Col de la Loze

Courchevel
FreeWinter ✓

Pass at 2,304 m between Courchevel and Méribel, reserved for bicycles: cars banned. 7 km on the Méribel side, 6.5 on the Courchevel side, a 23% wall.

See details →

Orelle – Cime Caron Gondola

Orelle
Winter ✓

Two-section link from Orelle to the Cime Caron, replacing the 3 Vallées Express. Summer 2026: 4 July – 30 August, 7 days a week, 09:45–16:15.

See details →

Saulire Cable Car

Courchevel
Winter ✓

Bottom station 2,077 m, top station 2,710 m. Rebuilt by POMA, reopened 6 December 2024. Summer 2026: 4 July – 30 August, 9:30am–4:30pm. Operated by Société des 3 Vallées.

See details →

Pointe de la Masse Gondola

Les Belleville
Winter ✓

Gondola to 2,804 m above Les Menuires, closed Thursday to Saturday inclusive. Glass-floored cabin and machine-room access included with the pedestrian ticket.

See details →

Mont Vallon Gondola

Les Allues
Winter ✓

Arrives at 2,952 m above Méribel, commune of Les Allues, in 8 minutes. Three lifts to chain from Méribel-Mottaret. Summer 2026: 4 July – 29 August.

See details →
