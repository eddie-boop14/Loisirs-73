What to do when it rains in Savoie?

Indoor outings in Savoie: museums and castles with interior visits, 2026 hours and prices verified.

When the rain sets in, Savoie has its interiors: seven museums (Savoisien and Beaux-Arts in Chambéry, Opinel, Espace Alu, the Cave Bear museum, Tremplin 92, Les Charmettes) and castles you visit inside (dukes of Savoie, Miolans, Redoute Marie-Thérèse).

Frequently asked questions

What to do in Chambéry when it rains?

The Musée Savoisien (free) and the fine-arts museum (free collections), Rousseau's house at Les Charmettes, and the guided tour of the dukes' castle are all under cover.

Which museums are free?

The Musée Savoisien, the fine-arts museum's permanent collections, Les Charmettes and the Opinel museum are free; the Espace Alu, the Cave Bear museum and Tremplin 92 charge.

Can you tour a castle in the rain?

Yes: the dukes' castle (guided tour), the château de Miolans and the Redoute Marie-Thérèse are visited inside. Seasonal hours are on each page.
