Frequently asked questions

What family outing on a fine day in Savoie?

Supervised swimming at Aix-les-Bains or Grésy-sur-Isère, the gorges du Sierroz on footbridges, or the flat, pushchair-friendly lakeside path of the lac du Bourget.

And when it rains, with children?

The Cave Bear museum at Entremont-le-Vieux, Tremplin 92 and its interactive modules in Albertville, or the Opinel museum at Saint-Jean-de-Maurienne.

Are there pushchair-friendly walks?

The lac du Bourget lakeside path is flat and accessible; the Hautecour art trail offers a 700 m 'Promenade Confort' variant.
