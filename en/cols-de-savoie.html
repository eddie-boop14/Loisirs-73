Savoie mountain passes: which to discover?

Savoie's road passes, grouped by valley — Tarentaise, Maurienne, Beaufortain. Opening and season from official sources.

Frequently asked questions

What is the highest road pass in Savoie?

The col de l'Iseran, at 2,764 m or 2,770 m depending on the official pages, is the highest road point, in the heart of the Vanoise National Park.

When are Savoie's passes open?

Most open from late May or June to October, depending on snow. Each page gives the published period and flags any contradictions between sources.

Do these passes charge?

No, access to the road passes is free. Only some nearby tunnels or lifts charge, which the place's page notes.
