What is Grande Motte Funicular

The Grande Motte funicular, known as the Perce-Neige, climbs from Val Claret towards the Grande Motte glacier at Tignes. It is almost entirely underground: 3,490 m of tunnel, except for a short middle section where two parallel bores let the trains pass each other. Secondary sources present it as the longest funicular in France.

Do not confuse it with the Grande Motte cable car, which takes over above it and has its own page. The funicular starts at Val Claret; the cable car does not.

Summer 2026: running from 20 June to 31 August, daily, on two timetables — 06:45 to 16:05 early in the season, 09:30 to 17:05 afterwards. No source publishes the date on which one gives way to the other.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Grande Motte funicular?

3,490 m, almost entirely in tunnel. Secondary sources present it as the longest funicular in France; no operator source states this.

Are the Grande Motte funicular and cable car the same machine?

No. The funicular runs from Val Claret up to the glacier; the Grande Motte cable car takes over above it. They are two machines in series, each with its own page.

When does the funicular run in summer 2026?

From 20 June to 31 August 2026, daily. Be careful: four other end-of-season dates circulate for the Grande Motte site, between 15 July and 6 September. Check before going up.

What time does the funicular open?

Two windows are published, 06:45–16:05 early season and 09:30–17:05 afterwards, but no source says when the switch happens.

How old is the funicular?

14 April 1993. Tunnelling ran from 13 December 1989 to 1 October 1991, after geological studies in 1987.
