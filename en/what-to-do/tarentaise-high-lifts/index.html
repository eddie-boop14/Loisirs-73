The Tarentaise up high: glaciers, funicular and cable cars

From the Grande Motte to the Bellecôte glacier, the lifts that climb highest in Savoie — and the five end-of-season dates circulating for a single site.

How this selection is made — Compiled from our verified fact sheets: this page's contents are exactly the output of the stated selector, never a hand-written list. It updates whenever a sheet changes.

The selection · 14

Aiguille Rouge Cable Car

Bourg-Saint-Maurice

Top station at 3,226 m per the operator, the highest point of the ski area. Summer 2026: open from 4 July. Pass Aiguille Rouge €16 / €13. Dogs allowed on a lead.

See details →

Cime Caron Cable Car

Les Belleville

From 2,320 m to about 3,190 m above Val Thorens. New cabins since 22 November 2025. Summer 2026: 4 July – 28 August, 9:45am–4:15pm, last ascent 3:45pm.

See details →

Vanoise Express

Peisey-Nancroix

Double-decker cable car between Plan-Peisey (1,612 m) and Montchavin (1,548 m). Summer 2026: 6 July – 28 August, Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday only. Pedestrian return €17.

See details →

Grande Motte Cable Car

Tignes

From 3,034 m to 3,456 m above Tignes, at the top of the Funiculaire Perce-Neige. Summer skiing 2026: 20 June – 15 July only. No dogs and no wheelchairs on the lifts.

See details →

Grande Motte Funicular

Tignes
Winter ✓

A 3,490 m funicular in tunnel, from Val Claret to the Grande Motte glacier. Summer 2026: 20 June – 31 August. Not to be confused with the cable car above it.

See details →

Champagny Gondola

Champagny-en-Vanoise
Winter ✓

Gondola at Champagny-en-Vanoise, at the gates of the Vanoise. Summer 2026: 5 July – 28 August, closed Saturdays, break 12:45–14:00. Ascent €9.

See details →

Pointe de la Masse Gondola

Les Belleville
Winter ✓

Gondola to 2,804 m above Les Menuires, closed Thursday to Saturday inclusive. Glass-floored cabin and machine-room access included with the pedestrian ticket.

See details →

Roche de Mio Gondola

La Plagne Tarentaise
Winter ✓

New gondola since December 2025, from Plagne Bellecôte to 2,739 m. Summer 2026: 4 July – 29 August, 09:00–17:00, closed Saturdays. Single ascent €9.

See details →

Solaise Gondola

Val-d'Isère
FreeWinter ✓

Gondola free for pedestrians to the Tête de Solaise, 2,560 m, above Val-d'Isère. No mountain bikes. Summer 2026: 4 July – 30 August.

See details →

Glaciers Gondola

La Plagne Tarentaise
Winter ✓

Pedestrian gondola above 3,000 m, extending Roche de Mio. Summer 2026: 4 July – 29 August, closed Saturdays. Single ascent €9.

See details →

Mont Vallon Gondola

Les Allues
Winter ✓

Arrives at 2,952 m above Méribel, commune of Les Allues, in 8 minutes. Three lifts to chain from Méribel-Mottaret. Summer 2026: 4 July – 29 August.

See details →

Olympique Cable Car

Val-d'Isère
Winter ✓

Val-d'Isère 3S cable car, free for pedestrians and €23 a day with a bike. Open 09:45–16:45 without a break, 4 July to 30 August 2026.

See details →

Saulire Cable Car

Courchevel

Bottom station 2,077 m, top station 2,710 m. Rebuilt by POMA, reopened 6 December 2024. Summer 2026: 4 July – 30 August, 9:30am–4:30pm. Operated by Société des 3 Vallées.

See details →

Mont-Bochor Cable Car

Pralognan-la-Vanoise
Winter ✓

Cable car from Pralognan to the Mont Bochor promontory and its orientation table at 2,023 m. Closed 12:45–13:30; in June it runs three days a week.
