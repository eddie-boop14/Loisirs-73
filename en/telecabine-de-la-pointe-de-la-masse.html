What is Pointe de la Masse Gondola

The Pointe de la Masse gondola climbs to 2,804 m above Les Menuires, in the commune of Les Belleville. It is a 10-seat detachable gondola — not a cable car, contrary to what is often written.

One fact governs everything else: it is closed from Thursday to Saturday inclusive. It runs Sunday to Wednesday only, while the rest of the Les Menuires area is open seven days a week. Les 3 Vallées state this plainly, and it is exactly the kind of detail that ruins a day out.

The operator advertises 1,000 m of ascent in 8 minutes, an optional glass-floored cabin, and machine-room access included with the pedestrian ticket.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the Pointe de la Masse served by a cable car?

No. It is a 10-seat detachable gondola (TCD10), described as a "gondola lift" by Les Menuires. A "Pointe de la Masse cable car" does not exist.

Which days does the gondola run?

Sunday to Wednesday only. It is closed from Thursday to Saturday inclusive, while the rest of the Les Menuires area runs 7 days a week.

How high does it go?

2,804 m. The operator advertises 1,000 m of ascent covered in 8 minutes.

Is there a glass-floored cabin?

Yes, a glass-floored cabin is offered. No source consulted puts a figure on the supplement.

How much does the ride cost?

One reading gives €26.00 for a single adult ascent and €19.50 with the ascent pass, but these are not confirmed on an operator pricing page.

When should you go?

Best time: Summer (4 July – 28 August 2026).
