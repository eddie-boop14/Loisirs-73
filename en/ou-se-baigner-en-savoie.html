Where to swim in Savoie?

Savoie's swimming spots ranked by real cost — entry and parking. 2026 lifeguard dates verified against official sources.

Savoie has large swimming lakes. Some beaches are free (Aqualac in Aix-les-Bains, lac de Carouge, lac Saint-André, Grésy-sur-Isère); others charge during the supervised season (Le Bourget-du-Lac, Le Sougey and Aiguebelette-le-Lac on the lac d'Aiguebelette).

Frequently asked questions

Which Savoie lakes have free swimming?

Aix-les-Bains beach (Aqualac), lac de Carouge, lac Saint-André and the Grésy-sur-Isère base are free. The lac d'Aiguebelette and Le Bourget-du-Lac beaches charge during the supervised season.

When is swimming supervised in 2026?

Most beaches are supervised from mid-June to late August; exact dates differ by site and sometimes between two official sources — each page states them.

Are there wheelchair-accessible beaches?

Yes, several pages note wheelchair access 'without assistance' or 'with help' per the tourist offices; the detail is on each relevant page.
