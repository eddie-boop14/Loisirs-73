Avrieux
Cascade de Saint-Benoît

A 90 m fall on the Esseillon forts tourist road, by the Saint-Benoît chapel. Free; road closed in winter, one-way uphill in summer, very limited parking.

Champagny-en-Vanoise

1 place
Free
Champagny-en-Vanoise
Cascade du Py

The Py waterfall at Champagny-en-Vanoise: an 80 m drop at Le Laisonnay, footbridge at its foot, free access and stated wheelchair-accessible unassisted. A protected valley at the Vanoise gateway.

Google Maps Official site

Courchevel

1 place
Free
Courchevel
Cascade des Poux

The Poux waterfall at Courchevel: 1 h on the botanical trail from the Rosière lake, an easy 2.7 km return, free, dogs accepted, 1 May to 31 October. Avoid in heavy rain.

Google Maps Official site

Grand-Aigueblanche

1 place
Free
Grand-Aigueblanche
Cascade du Morel

A near-80 m fall born of a diversion tunnel dug from 1902, on a trail that passes more than 50 cascades of the Morel. Free, June to September.

Google Maps Official site

Grésy-sur-Aix

Free
Grésy-sur-Aix
Gorges du Sierroz and cascade de Grésy

A rehabilitated canyon reopened in 2021: cantilevered walkways lead to the Grésy waterfall and Queen Hortense's 1813 stele. Free, year-round, wheelchair accessible.

Jacob-Bellecombette

1 place
Free
Jacob-Bellecombette
Cascades de Jacob et la Grobelle

Three waterfalls on the Nant du Pontet at Chambéry's gates: a 2 h 15 waymarked loop (5.9 km), giant's kettles, the Nymphs' Bath and George Sand. Finest after rain — the advice is official.

Le Châtelard

Free
Le Châtelard
Cascade du Pissieu

A tufa-forming waterfall in the Bauges fed by the Tanne du Cochon, one of Savoie's deepest chasms (825 m). Very easy 7.5 km loop, about 2h, free.

Planay

Planay
Cascade de la Vuzelle

The Vuzelle waterfall at Planay: around 100 m of drop, a site protected since 1935, trail from Chambéranger. The La Rochette approach is closed until further notice — both sources quoted.

Pralognan-la-Vanoise
Cascade de la Fraîche

Easy 1h30 loop from Place du Barioz in Pralognan-la-Vanoise to the Fraîche waterfall. Free, dogs allowed, open 15 May to 30 October.

Saint-Cassin

Free
Saint-Cassin
Cascade de Saint-Cassin

The Saint-Cassin waterfall, known as the Cascade de Couz: a stream detaching from the cliff on the Chartreuse foothills, admired by Rousseau, Lamartine and George Sand. Free access all year.

Google Maps Official site

Saint-Jean-d'Arvey

1 place
Free
Saint-Jean-d'Arvey
Grande cascade de la Doria

A 60 m fall above Chambéry: a 1-hour loop from the Chaffardon car park, with a corniche section and a cabled passage. Free, 1 May to 31 October, not advised in wet weather.

Google Maps Official site

Saint-Pierre-d'Entremont
Cirque de Saint-Même

Four waterfalls of the Guiers Vif at the head of a Chartreuse cirque. Paid parking in season (€5 per car), free access the rest of the year. Loops of 30 min and 1h30.
