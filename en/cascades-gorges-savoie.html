Most waterfalls are free to access; only the Cirque de Saint-Même has paid parking in season. The gorges du Sierroz run along footbridges, and the cascade de Saint-Benoît lines the Esseillon forts road.

Frequently asked questions

Which waterfall to see near Chambéry?

The grande cascade de la Doria overlooks Chambéry via a short loop, and the gorges du Sierroz at Grésy-sur-Aix run along footbridges — both are close to the city.

Are the waterfalls free?

Six of the seven are free. Only the Cirque de Saint-Même has paid parking in season; access to the site itself stays free.

Can you go with children?

The gorges du Sierroz and several short loops suit families; other waterfall trails need more care. Each page gives the timing and elevation.
